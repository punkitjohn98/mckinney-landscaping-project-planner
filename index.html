Begin With a Complete Property Walkthrough

Walk around the property at different times of day and record sunlight, shade, drainage patterns, slopes, compacted soil, struggling plants and areas affected by heavy foot traffic. Photograph existing beds, lawn areas, downspouts, irrigation equipment, utilities and access gates.

Think about how each outdoor area should function. Identify where people enter, gather, walk, play or need privacy. Separate immediate problems from optional improvements so the project can be organized into realistic priorities.

Coordinate the Main Landscape Elements

01

Landscape design

Create a complete layout before purchasing plants or beginning construction. Coordinate beds, lawns, drainage, irrigation, walkways and future outdoor-living features.

02

Plants and softscapes

Match plants to sun exposure, available space, soil conditions and watering needs. Use mature plant dimensions when determining spacing near buildings and walkways.

03

Drainage

Identify low areas, erosion and downspout discharge. Drainage improvements should be planned before new beds, lawns or outdoor surfaces are installed.

04

Irrigation

Separate watering zones according to plant and turf needs. Account for sunlight, soil, slopes and future landscape growth when planning sprinkler or drip coverage.

Plan the Work in a Logical Sequence

1. Site preparation

Remove unwanted material and protect vegetation that will remain.

2. Drainage

Correct grading and install necessary underground drainage.

3. Irrigation

Place lines, valves and sleeves before finished surfaces or beds.

4. Installation

Complete soil preparation, planting, mulch and final adjustments.

Homeowner Landscaping Checklist

  • Photograph the complete property.
  • Record sunlight and shaded areas.
  • Identify drainage and erosion problems.
  • Locate utilities and irrigation components.
  • Define how each outdoor area will be used.
  • Choose which existing features will remain.
  • Plan lawns, beds and walkways together.
  • Select plants for mature size and site conditions.
  • Coordinate irrigation with planting needs.
  • Confirm equipment and delivery access.
  • Organize work into practical phases.
  • Create a long-term maintenance plan.
